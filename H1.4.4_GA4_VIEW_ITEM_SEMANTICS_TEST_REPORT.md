## Coverage Finding (honest, not flattering)
None of the 70 inherited H1.4.3 suites actually exercise `analytics-platforms.js` /
`trackPlatformEvent` / `GA4_EVENT_MAP` / the storefront ecommerce tracking pipeline.
A keyword search surfaced 10 suites matching `view_product`/`add_to_cart` as strings,
but inspection showed these are all Geo-dashboard funnel field names
(`view_product_visitors`, `add_to_cart_visitors`) — a different subsystem, unrelated to
this bug. The file this bug lives in had **zero** pre-existing regression coverage;
the 2 new H1.4.4 suites are the first regression asset for it.

## Development Correction (recorded per request)
During investigation, a product-card click → `view_item` hook was prototyped
(`handleProductCardView()` in line-order.html, `bindProductViewDelegation()` in
line-shipping.html). Reality Audit proved product cards currently have no
product-detail interaction — no modal, drawer, detail page, or click reaction of any
kind. The tracking-only click hook was therefore rejected and fully reverted before
this gate. Final production does not invent a product-view interaction; both HTML
files are byte-identical to the H1.4.3 baseline.
