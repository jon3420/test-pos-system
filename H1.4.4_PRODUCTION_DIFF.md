## Change description

Removed an incorrect GA4 mapping: internal `view_product` (a product-card viewport
impression signal) was being translated into GA4 ecommerce `view_item`. This mapping
is removed. `view_product` continues to flow to internal `/api/analytics/events`
logging and to Meta `ViewContent` exactly as before (baseline contract, unchanged).

No `view_item` GA4 mapping exists after this change — there is currently no legitimate
product-detail-view interaction in the codebase to attach it to (see Reality Audit
§6). This is intentional.
