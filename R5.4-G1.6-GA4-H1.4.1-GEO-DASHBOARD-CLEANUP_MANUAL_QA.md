# R5.4-G1.6-GA4-H1.4.1-GEO-DASHBOARD-CLEANUP — Manual QA

To be performed after real deployment, in an actual browser. Automated
gates (Target Runtime, Static Audit, Full Regression) all pass, but that is
not a substitute for this checklist — see note at the end.

## MAP — Wheel interaction

- **MAP-01** — On Dashboard, put the mouse over the map and scroll. Expect:
  the page scrolls normally; the map does **not** zoom.
- **MAP-02** — Click the map once. Expect: a small hint appears (or
  changes) and wheel zoom becomes active.
- **MAP-03** — Click inside the map again while already active. Expect:
  still active (does not toggle off).
- **MAP-04** — Press Esc. Expect: wheel zoom deactivates, hint resets to
  "點擊地圖後可使用滾輪縮放".
- **MAP-05** — Click the map again. Expect: active again.
- **MAP-06** — Click somewhere outside the map (not the +/- buttons).
  Expect: wheel zoom deactivates.
- **MAP-07** — Click the Leaflet `+`/`-` zoom buttons while wheel zoom is
  inactive. Expect: they work normally regardless of wheel state.
- **MAP-08** — Switch to the Heatmap tab. Expect: wheel zoom is active
  immediately, no click needed.
- **MAP-09** — Switch back to Dashboard. Expect: wheel zoom is inactive
  again (does not carry over from Heatmap).

## UI — Dashboard cleanliness

- **UI-01** — Dashboard shows no "Unknown" ratio anywhere.
- **UI-02** — Dashboard shows no "Geo Quality" badge/section.
- **UI-03** — Dashboard shows no Acquisition Geo warning text (e.g. "目前所
  有訪客皆為未知區域").
- **UI-04** — Dashboard shows no Recommended Actions / Decision Center /
  Business Opportunity block.
- **UI-05** — Dashboard shows no old 8-metric selector buttons (訪客/加入購
  物車/開始結帳/完成訂單/營收/成交率/購物車放棄/建議風險).
- **UI-06** — Dashboard shows no legacy Top-3 sections or ranking table.
- **UI-07** — Dashboard shows no "暫無資料" or "目前沒有符合條件的區域資料"
  text.
