## Empty range / Sync CTA
- **SYNC-01** Select a range that has never been synced. Old markers disappear immediately; status shows "此區間尚未同步 GA4 區域資料" with a "立即同步並顯示" button.
- **SYNC-02** Click the CTA. Button shows "同步中…". On completion, markers appear automatically — no tab switch, no F5.
- **SYNC-03** Force a sync failure (e.g. disconnect network briefly). Button is not stuck on "同步中…"; it's clickable again and a retry succeeds normally.
- **SYNC-04** A sync that succeeds but the range genuinely has zero GA4 rows: map stays empty, CTA remains available to retry, no stale old markers reappear.

## Sync concurrency
- **SYNC-RACE-01** Start a 7d sync, then switch to 30d before it finishes. 30d must NOT show "同步中…" (that belongs to 7d).
- **SYNC-RACE-02** With 7d sync still in flight, click 30d's CTA. Expect: no second sync fires; a busy message ("另一個區間正在同步中，請稍候再試") appears instead.
- **SYNC-RACE-03** Let the 7d sync finish while 30d is still selected. Expect: the view stays on 30d — it must NOT jump back to 7d, and 30d's title/markers must be unaffected.

## Dashboard wheel
- **WHEEL-D-01** On Dashboard, scroll the mouse wheel over the map without clicking first: the page scrolls, the map does not zoom.
- **WHEEL-D-02** Click the map, then scroll: the map zooms.
- **WHEEL-D-03** Press Esc: wheel locks again.
- **WHEEL-D-04** Click outside the map: wheel locks again.

## Heatmap wheel
- **WHEEL-H-01** Switch to Heatmap, scroll without clicking: page scrolls, map does not zoom.
- **WHEEL-H-02** Click the map, then scroll: map zooms.
- **WHEEL-H-03** Esc: locks.
- **WHEEL-H-04** Click outside: locks.
- **WHEEL-H-05** Switch to Dashboard and back to Heatmap: each entry starts locked again, regardless of the state you left it in.

## DOM-replacement wheel bug (Production Bug #2) — **this one matters most, test it deliberately**
- **WHEEL-REFRESH-01** On Dashboard: click the map (wheel enables). Then trigger anything that causes the Dashboard GA4 block to re-render — switching a date range, or completing a Sync — and wait for it to finish. Afterward: wheel should be locked again (expected, since re-activation always relocks). Click the map again: it **must** successfully enable wheel-zoom this time too. If clicking after a refresh silently does nothing, that is exactly the bug that was found and fixed this round — flag immediately if seen.
