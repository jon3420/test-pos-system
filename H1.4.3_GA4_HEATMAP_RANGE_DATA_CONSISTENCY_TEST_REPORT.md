## NON-FINAL TRANSIENT TEST HARNESS OBSERVATION

During development of the H1.4.3 regression runner, one inherited legacy
suite — `scripts/smoke-hotfix30-b5-r5-3-a7-geo-kpi-single-source.js`
(originally added in H1.4.1, unrelated to any H1.4.3 change) — failed once
(1 of 87 assertions, "B4-2: 不得 fallback 顯示舊 getGeoFunnel() fixture 的
數字（42）") during one full-regression sequence.

Handling:
- No override was added to the strict classifier.
- The strict classifier was not relaxed in any way.
- The suite was re-run standalone in isolation immediately afterward: 4/4
  clean runs, no failures.
- A full fresh H1.4.3 regression sequence was re-run afterward and produced
  clean, byte-identical results across 3 independent rounds (70/70 ×3,
  reported above).
- H1.4.3's own three new suites are appended at the **end** of the SUITE
  array, strictly after this inherited suite in execution order — so they
  cannot be the source of any state that leaked *forward* into it.

This is consistent with this codebase's own long-documented history of
intermittent, order-sensitive legacy test flakiness (see
`regression_flaky_note.txt`, entries going back to the original R5.4-G1
round). Per instruction, this is recorded precisely rather than
minimized: **not reproduced after isolation and three subsequent strict
full-regression rounds.** This is not the same claim as "definitely
unrelated to all H1.4.3 changes" — it is the strongest claim the evidence
actually supports.
